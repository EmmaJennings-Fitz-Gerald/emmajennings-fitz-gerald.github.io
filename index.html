Emma Jennings-Fitz-Gerald

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Sociology at the University of Toronto, St. George Campus.

I specialize in gender, violence, military sociology, and work. I have experience with research on sexual misconduct in the Canadian Armed Forces, gender-based violence in the Canadian craft beer industry, and intimate partner violence in 2SLGBTQQIA+ relationships.

Outside of research I spend my time reading, weightlifting, swimming, watching new shows and films, and visiting new places with my partner and friends.
